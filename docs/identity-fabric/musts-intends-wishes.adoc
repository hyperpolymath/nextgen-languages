= nextgen-languages — Normative Scope (Must / Intend / Wish)
:toc:
:revdate: 2026-09-26

This document states what the coordinator is responsible for. It does not define the individual languages or assert unverified integration with adjacent projects.

== Levels

* *MUST* — structural requirement for this repository.
* *INTEND* — committed direction for coordinator work.
* *WISH* — aspiration, not a present capability.

== Scope

[cols="1,5", options="header"]
|===
| Level | Requirement

| *MUST*
| Remain a *pure coordinator*: index canonical language repositories without vendoring language implementations, grammars, proofs, or detailed per-language documentation.

| *MUST*
| Keep the canonical registry, human pointers, boundary guard list, ecosystem language keys, and status tracker consistent; verify with `bash hooks/validate-language-registry.sh`.

| *MUST*
| Distinguish core portfolio membership from syntax surfaces, tools, curricula, libraries, targets, and adjacent research; do not imply maturity or integration from mere inclusion or linking.

| *MUST*
| Respect privacy boundaries. The private 007 and exploratory TypeFix Zero entries remain index-only; do not disclose private source, URLs, or details.

| *INTEND*
| Keep the README, EXPLAINME, wiki, and ecosystem map usable as an entry path for people who do not already know the repository topology.

| *INTEND*
| Record status claims with dates and evidence boundaries. A `TBD` value means “not verified here”; it is not a readiness grade.

| *INTEND*
| Add newly discovered projects only after verifying their canonical URL, owner, scope, and privacy policy. Resolve “Ziz” and the `jtv-lang` lineage only with owner-confirmed information.

| *WISH*
| A useful, low-maintenance graph of the broader language ecosystem with named relationship types and links to authoritative upstream evidence.

| *WISH*
| Shared conformance tooling where multiple project owners explicitly adopt and maintain it.
|===

== Relationship boundaries

The portfolio is related by research questions; it is not a unified compiler or runtime. Links in the ecosystem map are classified as dependency, shared substrate, syntax surface, tool, curriculum, or research adjacency. Only call something an integration when a tested dependency, adapter, or conformance path supports the claim.

Tangle is an independent topological language. Its own canonical repository is the authority for its representation and scope; this coordinator does not assert a KRL dependency or make KRL/QuandleDB a member of this registry. Similarly, `typed-wasm` and `echo-types` are discoverable shared-infrastructure projects, not language-family members. Consult the link:../ecosystem-map.adoc[ecosystem map] for current pointers and caveats.
