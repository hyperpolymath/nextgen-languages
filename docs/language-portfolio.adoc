== Core portfolio

The registry has 17 records: 16 committed entries with human pointers, plus one exploratory/private TypeFix Zero record not yet committed to the family. The committed entries span active projects, experimental language work, a continuity research prototype, and a private index-only item. “In the registry” is not a readiness grade. Consult the individual project and the dated link:audits/2026-09-07-language-portfolio.md[portfolio audit] for evidence and caveats.

Grouping is a reading aid, not a formal taxonomy. My-Lang's Solo, Duet, and Ensemble are nested dialects; “Me” is a runtime projection, not a fourth dialect. KitchenSpeak, Firmboot, 007, and TypeFix Zero have distinct project/status boundaries documented in the machine registry.

== Related work is not automatically a member

The AffineScript syntax-surface projects (CafeScripto, JaffaScript, LucidScript, PseudoScript, RattleScript), Kategoria, Panoply, typed-wasm, echo-types, selected `*-iser` tools, and education projects are deliberately classified separately. Follow their roles and links in the link:ecosystem-map.adoc[ecosystem map].

The separate `hyperpolymath/jtv-lang` repository describes related JtV work, but its lineage against canonical `hyperpolymath/jtv` is unresolved here. “Ziz” was not identified as a public language repository in the Hyperpolymath or Metadatastician estate search on 2026-09-26; no speculative entry is made.
