== Scope and status

The registry's `status` field is not a standards readiness grade. The coordinator records no family-wide completion percentage. `TBD` means no value is verified here; implementations and current readiness evidence live in each owning project.

Separate database and type-theory estates—including the KRL resolution stack, VeriSimDB, Quandledb, TypeLL, and PanLL—are out of this registry's scope. Tangle is an independent language and is not represented here as a KRL dependency. See the adjacent-project section in link:docs/ecosystem-map.adoc[the ecosystem map] for qualified pointers.

== Update protocol

When a committed family member is added, removed, renamed, or extracted, update the registry surfaces together and run `bash hooks/validate-language-registry.sh`. For adjacent surfaces, tools, libraries, targets, education projects, or research neighbors, update `docs/ecosystem-map.adoc` and the `[portfolio-adjacent]` section of `ECOSYSTEM.a2ml`; do not enlarge the core family by default. Then run `bash hooks/validate-coordinator-boundary.sh`.
